3.Framework-by-framework verdict
1) LangGraph
Best for

.agentic RAG
.multi-agent systems
.routing between agents
.retries and self-correction
.stateful workflows
.production orchestration

Why
LangGraph is designed as a low-level orchestration framework for stateful agents and highlights durable execution, memory, and human oversight. That is extremely useful when your system has planner, retriever, evaluator, citation checker, note-writer, and tool-using agents.
My take
For the agent layer, this is the best choice.

2) LlamaIndex
Best for
.document ingestion
.parsing PDFs and structured docs
.chunking/indexing
.retrieval pipelines
.source-grounded document Q&A

Why
LlamaIndex is now heavily centered on document agents, parsing, indexing, structured extraction, and agentic document workflows. That makes it a very natural fit for a NotebookLM-like app where documents are the center of the experience.

My take
For the RAG/document layer, this is the best choice.
